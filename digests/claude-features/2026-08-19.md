## 5. OpenAI pauses its next frontier model, Astra, after hitting a "Critical" cyber-capability threshold [Other AI]
High confidence, corroborated across Forbes, The Register, IT Pro, and OpenAI's own blog posts (Aug 8-19). OpenAI paused RL training and evaluation on Astra after preliminary testing showed it crossing a "Critical" cybersecurity threshold — assessed as potentially able to independently find zero-days or run novel attacks against hardened systems. The fix is a new chain-of-thought monitoring regime (inspecting the model's reasoning trace for high-risk activity, 30-minute alert-and-halt requirement) that OpenAI says adds roughly 20% inference-compute overhead on covered workloads. Notable as a lab actually pausing on a pre-committed capability threshold rather than just publishing a policy — pure industry awareness, no direct project tie-in.
