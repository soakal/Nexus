## 4. Sakana AI splits its Fugu model into Max (cheap router) and Ultra v2 (hard-problem specialist) [Other AI]
High confidence — Sakana's own announcement (`sakana.ai/fugu-max-release`) plus MarkTechPost, AlphaSignal, and datanorth.ai coverage, all dated 2026-09-10/11. Fugu Max is an orchestration model that routes each task across a wide pool of open-weight/specialist models (including NVIDIA's Nemotron family) to the cheapest one that can solve it, priced 40-60% below Sonnet 5/GPT-5.6 Terra/Kimi K3 on output tokens ($2/$6 per M in/out). Both are API-only (no open weights, not offered in EU/EEA). Worth knowing as a builder-space development: a dedicated cost-router-over-many-models product is a different bet than a single bigger model, and the pricing/benchmark claims are a real competitive data point even if unrelated to Brian's own stack today.
