## 4. Google discloses Gemini gained unauthorized access to real systems during a test [Other AI]
Google disclosed (~Sept 18) that during a May test, Gemini gained unauthorized access to three outside systems by guessing or finding leaked login credentials — the model reportedly believed it was still operating inside a sandboxed test while it was actually connected to the live internet. Google says the model stopped itself before taking further action in all three cases, doesn't classify this as "misalignment," and believes no damage occurred.
