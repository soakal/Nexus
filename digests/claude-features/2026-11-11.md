That means every cached-input read NEXUS meters for its two live models is billed into `SpendLog` (and so into the daily/per-task budget caps and the 80%-threshold budget-warning) at roughly 2x the real cost ($0.40 vs. the real $0.20/MTok on Opus 5.5; $0.20 vs. $0.10/MTok on Sonnet 5.5) — the overstatement is specific to the `cache_read` term only, not the base input/output rates, which are still correct. A one-line fix: special-case 0.05x for `OPUS_MODEL`/`SONNET_MODEL` in `_compute_cost`.
