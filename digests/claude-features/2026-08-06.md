## 3. Trust the model's judgement — thinner prompts, self-selected subagent model tiers [New technique]
From Simon Willison's July 21 fireside-chat writeup with two Claude Code team members (@_catwu, @trq212) — moderate-high confidence, direct quotes from Anthropic staff. Their advice: stop over-loading prompts with examples and "don't do this" lists (Claude reportedly performs better without them, and Claude Code's own system prompt reportedly shrank ~80% on this philosophy), and for subagent delegation, phrase it as "use your judgement to decide an appropriate lower power model and run that in a subagent" rather than hardcoding the tier yourself. Directly applicable to how Workflow scripts and Council-loop prompts are written today (both currently hardcode agent/model tiers per step) — worth trying a version of a workflow prompt that lets the model pick Haiku vs Sonnet per subtask itself instead of you pre-assigning it.
